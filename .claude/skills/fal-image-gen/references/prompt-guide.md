# 提示詞擴寫對照表

擴寫時從這裡挑具體的英文詞彙，避免寫出「beautiful」「high quality」這類沒有畫面的形容詞。

## 五個面向的檢查清單

擴寫完檢查一遍，五格都有東西才送出：

- [ ] **主題**：是誰／是什麼？表情、姿勢、情緒、服裝、道具都寫了嗎？
- [ ] **構圖**：主體佔畫面多少？在哪個位置？相機從哪個角度看？留白在哪？
- [ ] **場景**：背景是哪裡？什麼光？什麼氛圍？有沒有煙霧／水滴／塵埃這類細節？
- [ ] **風格**：什麼藝術風格？主色調？材質質感？
- [ ] **鏡頭**：焦段光圈？景深深淺？

## 相機視角 Camera angle

| 中文 | 英文 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| 大特寫 | extreme close-up | 只有臉部局部、質感強烈 |
| 特寫 | close-up shot | 頭肩，情緒最清楚 |
| 中景 | medium shot | 腰部以上，人物＋部分環境 |
| 全身 | full body shot | 完整人物與姿態 |
| 遠景／大遠景 | wide shot / extreme wide shot | 環境為主，人物很小 |
| 仰視 | low angle shot | 主體顯得高大、有壓迫感 |
| 俯視 | high angle shot | 主體顯得渺小、脆弱 |
| 正上方俯拍 | top-down / overhead shot | 平面感、適合物件排列 |
| 過肩 | over-the-shoulder shot | 有敘事感、帶入視角 |
| 荷蘭角 | dutch angle | 傾斜、不安定感 |

## 鏡頭 Lens

| 焦段 | 適合 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| `14–24mm f/8` | 風景、建築、大場景 | 廣角、誇張透視、全景深 |
| `35mm f/2` | 環境人像、街拍 | 人＋環境的平衡感 |
| `50mm f/1.8` | 通用、日常人像 | 接近人眼、自然散景 |
| `85mm f/1.4` | 人像特寫 | 壓縮感、奶油散景、主體突出 |
| `100mm f/2.8 macro` | 微距、質感細節 | 極淺景深、細節放大 |
| `200mm f/2.8` | 遠距抓拍、運動 | 強壓縮、背景完全糊化 |

景深寫法：`shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh`（淺）／`deep focus, everything sharp`（深）。

## 範例：從一句話到完整提示詞

**使用者說**：一隻貓在咖啡廳

**擴寫後（英文，送給模型）**：
> A fluffy ginger tabby cat sitting upright on a worn wooden café table, ears perked forward,
> amber eyes fixed on the viewer with calm curiosity, one paw resting beside a half-finished
> latte with visible foam art. Medium close-up, cat occupying the left third of the frame,
> generous negative space on the right where warm window light spills in. Background: a softly
> blurred café interior, hanging pendant lamps, a barista silhouetted at the counter, faint
> steam rising from the cup, dust motes floating in the backlight. Golden hour side lighting,
> warm amber and muted brown palette, cozy and unhurried mood. Photorealistic editorial
> photography, shot on 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh. No text, no watermark.

**中文翻譯（給使用者確認）**：
> 一隻蓬鬆的橘色虎斑貓端坐在磨損的木頭咖啡桌上，耳朵向前豎起，琥珀色的眼睛平靜好奇地看著鏡頭，
> 一隻前爪擱在一杯喝了一半、還看得見拉花的拿鐵旁。中特寫，貓佔畫面左側三分之一，
> 右側留出大片空間讓溫暖的窗光灑進來。背景是柔焦的咖啡廳內部、吊燈、櫃檯後咖啡師的剪影，
> 杯口有淡淡蒸氣，逆光中有塵埃漂浮。黃金時刻側光，暖琥珀與霧棕色調，氣氛慵懶溫馨。
> 寫實編輯攝影風格，85mm f/1.4 拍攝，淺景深奶油散景。畫面無文字、無浮水印。

**參數**：模型 nano-banana-2 ／ 接口 text-to-image ／ 比例 16:9 ／ 張數 1 ／ 解析度 1K

# 影片提示詞詞彙

影片跟圖片最大的差別：圖片寫「一個瞬間」，影片要寫「一段時間內的變化」。
每個提示詞至少要回答三個問題：**誰在動、環境有什麼在動、相機怎麼動。**

## 相機運鏡 Camera movement

| 中文 | 英文 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| 固定機位 | static shot, locked-off tripod | 讓觀眾專注在畫面內的動作 |
| 緩慢推近 | slow dolly in / push in | 情緒逐漸聚焦、緊張感累積 |
| 拉遠 | dolly out / pull back | 揭露環境、情緒釋放 |
| 橫移 | tracking shot, lateral dolly | 跟著主體平行移動 |
| 環繞 | orbit / arc around the subject | 立體感、英雄式呈現 |
| 手持跟拍 | handheld follow, subtle camera shake | 臨場感、紀錄片感 |
| 升降 | crane up / crane down, jib move | 大景轉小景，開場或收尾 |
| 甩鏡 | whip pan | 快速轉場、能量感 |
| 搖鏡 | pan left / pan right, tilt up / tilt down | 掃視環境或身形 |
| 空拍 | aerial drone shot, flying over | 大範圍地景 |
| 變焦推鏡 | slow zoom in | 比推軌更平面，帶點復古感 |
| 移焦 | rack focus from A to B | 把注意力從前景交給背景 |
| 主觀鏡頭 | POV shot | 代入角色視角 |

## 動作與表情變化 Performance

寫「從 A 變成 B」，不要只寫狀態：

- `her guarded frown slowly softens into a half-smile as she looks up`
- `he exhales, shoulders dropping, then turns his head sharply toward the door`
- `the cat's ears flick back, tail twitching once before it leaps off the table`
- `fingers tighten around the cup, knuckles paling, then relax`

常用動態動詞：`turns`, `leans in`, `exhales`, `flinches`, `reaches for`, `steps forward`,
`glances over the shoulder`, `blinks slowly`, `tilts the head`, `brushes hair back`,
`smiles faintly`, `eyes widen`, `shifts weight`

節奏詞：`slowly`, `gradually`, `abruptly`, `in one fluid motion`, `mid-stride`,
`then`, `as`, `while`, `just before`

## 環境的動態元素 Living environment

`steam curling from the cup`, `rain streaking down the window`, `curtains billowing in the breeze`,
`dust motes drifting through the light beam`, `traffic sliding past in the background`,
`crowd flowing around the subject`, `leaves rustling`, `neon signs flickering`,
`candle flame wavering`, `snow falling softly`, `waves rolling in`,
`shadows creeping across the wall as clouds pass`

## 影片風格 Style

`cinematic live action, shallow depth of field, anamorphic flare`,
`documentary handheld, natural light, slight grain`,
`2D anime, cel shading, expressive keyframes`,
`stop-motion, visible fingerprints on clay`,
`3D render, soft global illumination`,
`vintage 16mm film, gate weave and grain`,
`music-video look, high contrast, saturated colour`

## 範例：從一句話到完整影片提示詞

**使用者說**：一個女生在雨天的窗邊喝咖啡

**擴寫後（英文，送給模型）**：
> A young woman sits by a rain-streaked café window, both hands wrapped around a steaming ceramic
> cup. She stares out at the street, expression distant and tired; then she blinks slowly, lifts the
> cup, takes a sip, and a small, private smile spreads as warmth reaches her. Rain runs in uneven
> rivulets down the glass, headlights of passing cars smear into soft bokeh behind her, and steam
> curls upward from the cup. The camera begins in a medium side profile and pushes in slowly to a
> close-up as she smiles. Overcast blue-grey daylight from the window, warm amber lamp glow on her
> face, cinematic live action, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain.

**中文翻譯（給使用者確認）**：
> 一位年輕女子坐在被雨水沖刷的咖啡廳窗邊，雙手捧著一杯冒著熱氣的陶瓷杯。她望著街道，
> 神情疏離而疲憊；接著緩緩眨眼、舉杯啜飲，暖意襲來時嘴角漾開一抹私密的微笑。
> 雨水在玻璃上蜿蜒流下，背後經過的車燈化成柔軟的散景光斑，杯口熱氣裊裊上升。
> 鏡頭從中景側面開始，隨著她的微笑緩緩推近成特寫。窗外是陰天的藍灰色日光，
> 檯燈的暖琥珀光落在她臉上，電影實拍質感，50mm 鏡頭，淺景深，帶細微顆粒。

**參數**：kling-v3-pro ／ text-to-video ／ 16:9 ／ 5 秒 ／ 含原生音訊
